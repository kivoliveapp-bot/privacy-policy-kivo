Kivo Live Privacy Policy

Kivo Live AI LLC
Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Kivo Live ("Kivo Live", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Kivo Live collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Kivo Live mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in to Kivo Live using Google, we may receive:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture
  • Google account identifier
  • Kivo Live account information

Profile and Social Information

We may process information related to your use of social features, including:

  • Username
  • Profile picture
  • Followers and following
  • Online status
  • User reports
  • Blocked users
  • Other information voluntarily added to your profile

Live Streaming

When you start or participate in a live stream, Kivo Live may access:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Live audio
  • Live video

Kivo Live uses Agora to provide real-time audio and video communication.

Camera and microphone access are used when you use features that require live audio or video.

Chat

Kivo Live provides text chat between users. We may process messages that you voluntarily send through the application's chat features.

Kivo Live does not provide photo or video attachments through its user chat feature.

Virtual Coins, Gifts and Earnings

Kivo Live provides virtual currency and gifting features. We may process information relating to:

  • Coins purchased by users
  • Virtual gifts sent or received
  • Gift history
  • User balances
  • Host earnings
  • Withdrawal information
  • Transactions related to virtual currency

Payments

Users may purchase virtual coins using real money. Payments may be processed through Google Play and, where applicable, authorized Kivo Live recharge agents.

Payment providers process payment information according to their own privacy policies and applicable payment rules.

Kivo Live does not need to store your complete payment card number when the payment is processed by an authorized payment provider.

Device and Technical Information

We may collect or process technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device identifier where permitted
  • Device type
  • Android version
  • Application version
  • Technical and diagnostic information
  • Information required for security and fraud prevention

Location Information

Kivo Live may use location information to display a user's general country or location.

Kivo Live does not use location information for continuous precise-location tracking.

Photos and Storage

Kivo Live may request access to photos or device storage when required for supported application features, such as selecting or updating profile content.

We do not use this permission to access unrelated files for purposes unrelated to the application's features.

Notifications

Kivo Live may request notification permission so that we can send relevant application notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide live audio and video streaming
  • Provide chat and social features
  • Display user profiles
  • Display followers and following
  • Display online status
  • Display a user's general country or location
  • Process virtual coins and gifts
  • Maintain gift history
  • Manage host earnings and withdrawals
  • Process purchases and recharge operations
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Process reports and blocking requests
  • Protect the security of users and the application
  • Maintain and improve Kivo Live
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

3. Third-Party Services

Kivo Live uses third-party services to provide certain features.

Google

Google services may be used for Google Sign-In and Google Play purchases.

Firebase

Kivo Live uses Firebase services for application functionality, such as authentication, database and other Firebase services enabled by Kivo Live.

Agora

Agora is used to provide real-time audio and video communication for live streaming.

These third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

4. App Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Kivo Live may request access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos or device storage
  • Notifications
  • Location

Permissions are requested only when they are needed for applicable features.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may share or provide access to information when necessary to:

  • Operate Kivo Live
  • Provide third-party services
  • Process payments
  • Provide live audio and video communication
  • Maintain application security
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Respond to lawful requests
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect users and Kivo Live

6. Public and User-Generated Content

Kivo Live allows users to create and share content through profiles, live streams, chats, and social features.

Information that you choose to make publicly visible may be viewed by other Kivo Live users.

You should not publicly share passwords, payment information, private identification documents, or other sensitive personal information.

7. User Safety

Kivo Live provides features that allow users to:

  • Report other users
  • Block other users
  • Manage social interactions

Reports may be reviewed and processed for safety, moderation, fraud prevention, and enforcement of Kivo Live rules.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

We use appropriate security measures when transmitting information between the application and our services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide Kivo Live services
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Maintain transaction and virtual currency records
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain security
  • Resolve disputes
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our policies

10. Account and Data Deletion

Kivo Live provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Users may request account deletion through the account deletion functionality provided by Kivo Live or through our designated account deletion request process.

When an account deletion request is completed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized where applicable.

Certain information may be retained when necessary for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Financial or transaction records
  • Dispute resolution
  • Enforcement of our policies

11. Children's Privacy

Kivo Live is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. International Data Processing

Kivo Live and its third-party service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable measures to protect personal information during such processing.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
